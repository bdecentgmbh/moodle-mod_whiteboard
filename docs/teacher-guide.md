# Whiteboard — Teacher guide

A whiteboard brings a board of an online whiteboard service, such as Miro or TaskCards, into your
course. Students find it on the course page like any other activity and work on it together,
without looking for links in announcements or emails.

The board itself stays in the whiteboard service: you create and share it there, and everything on
it is kept there. Which services you can use is decided by your administrator.

## Adding a whiteboard

1. Create the board in the whiteboard service and share it so that your students can open it, for
   example with "anyone with the link can edit". Whether students need an account of their own
   depends on the service and on how you share the board.
2. Copy the link of the board, or its embed code if the service has one. The link in your browser's
   address bar while the board is open usually works.
3. In your course, turn editing on and add a **Whiteboard** activity.
4. Enter a name, and paste the link or the embed code into **Board link**. Below the field, you see
   which services are supported on your site.
5. Save. The whiteboard keeps only the link of the board, also when you paste an embed code.

If the link is not from a supported service, the form tells you which services are supported.

## What students see

- **In the page**: most boards are shown right in the activity page. Above the board,
  **Theatre mode** shows nothing but the board, its name and buttons to open it in a new tab and to
  exit, for working on a large board. **Open in a new tab** helps when the service asks to sign in.
- **In a new tab**: some services do not allow other websites to show their boards. For those, the
  activity page has an **Open the board** button instead.

Your administrator sets which of the two each service uses.

## When a board does not show

- **The board stays empty or shows an error**: check the sharing settings of the board in the
  service, and try **Open in a new tab**. Some services only show boards to people who are signed
  in, or only to members of your team.
- **"This board cannot be shown"**: the service of the link is not enabled on your site (any more).
  Change the board link in the settings of the activity, or ask your administrator.

Whiteboard services change their websites and plans from time to time, which can stop boards from
showing in other websites. If a board that worked before does not show any more, tell your
administrator.

## Completion

Students can complete the whiteboard by viewing it. What they do on the board is not tracked, as it
happens in the whiteboard service.

## Boards for groups and single students

A whiteboard can give each group, or single students, a board of their own. You add them on the
**Boards** page of the whiteboard, next to *Settings* at the top of the activity:

1. Create the boards in the whiteboard service and share them.
2. Open the whiteboard and go to **Boards**. Choose **Group boards** or **User boards** in the menu.
3. Press **Add a group board** or **Add a user board**, choose the group or the student, and paste
   the link or embed code of their board. **Save and add another** saves and starts the next one.

The list shows each group's or student's board, with links to open the boards in a new tab, and
buttons to change or delete them. Deleting a board here does not delete it in the whiteboard
service.

Which board someone sees:

1. A student with a **user board** sees it, and no group selector.
2. Otherwise, in group mode, students see the board of their group. With *Visible groups*, they can
   also switch to the boards of the other groups. You switch between the groups with the group
   selector above the board.
3. Everyone else sees the board link of the whiteboard: groups without a board, students without a
   group or user board, *All participants*, and everybody when the whiteboard is not in group mode.

Group boards are only used in group mode: choose *Separate groups* or *Visible groups* as **Group
mode** in the settings of the whiteboard, under *Common module settings*. If the whiteboard has a
grouping, only the groups of the grouping can have a board.

Whoever has the link of a board can usually open it, depending on how it is shared in the service.
With *Separate groups*, students only get the link of their own group's board; with *Visible
groups*, they get the links of all groups' boards. A user board is only shown to its student.

When you duplicate the whiteboard or import it into another course, the group boards are copied for
the same groups (or their copies); user boards are not. Resetting the course can delete the user
boards.
