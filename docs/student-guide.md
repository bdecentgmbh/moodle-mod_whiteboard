# Whiteboard — Student guide

A whiteboard is a board of an online whiteboard service, such as Miro or TaskCards, that your teacher
has added to the course. You open it like any other activity.

## Working on the board

- Most boards are shown right in the activity page. Use **Open in a new tab** below the board to
  work on it in a larger window.
- Some boards open in a new tab with **Open the board**, because the service does not allow other
  websites to show them.
- Depending on the service and on how your teacher shared the board, you may have to sign in to the
  service, or you may only be able to look at the board. Your teacher tells you if you need an
  account.

Everything you add to the board is kept by the whiteboard service, not by Moodle, and can usually be
seen by everyone who can open the board.

## When the board does not show

Try **Open in a new tab**. If the board does not show there either, or you see "This board cannot be
shown at the moment", contact your teacher.
